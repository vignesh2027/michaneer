For homes and hostels with an RO purifier

How much water does your RO send down the drain?

Every RO purifier has a second hose. Official figures put its output anywhere from 1 to 3 litres for every litre you drink, so the only way to know yours is to measure it. A bottle, a bucket and a measuring jug take about ten minutes.

The bucket test

  1. Empty the tank. Fill bottles from the drinking tap until only a thin stream is left. That stream is the membrane's live output.
  2. Start together. Keep the tap open, put a 1 litre bottle under it and the reject hose into an empty bucket at the same moment.
  3. Stop when the bottle is full. Pull the hose out, pour the bucket into a measuring jug and note the millilitres.
  4. Optional. A TDS pen reading of tap water and of purified water, and the rated recovery printed on the purifier's label.
